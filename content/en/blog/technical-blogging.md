A technical article can remain useful long after publication if its purpose and context are easy to find. Antonio Cangiano's _Technical Blogging_ examines how individual posts become a focused archive through topic selection, publishing habits, and ongoing maintenance.

## Building a Body of Work

The book covers choosing a focus, understanding readers, finding topics, publishing consistently, search and discovery, credibility, community, and maintaining a blog. Writing and reaching the reader belong to the same practice.

Its attention to future readers is especially relevant to engineering content. Titles, summaries, examples, and links make a post's purpose visible. A clear focus lets an archive cover different subjects without becoming a collection of unrelated announcements.

## A Sustainable Publishing Practice

Developers starting a blog or giving an existing one more direction will find practical guidance. The approach fits Mohetios.dev's purpose: keeping projects, reading, decisions, and experiments connected, with enough attention to discovery for useful work to be found.
